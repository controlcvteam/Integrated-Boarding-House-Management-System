AI-Assisted Backlog Review

Purpose:
An AI assistant was used as a thinking partner to review the backlog, identify missing CRUD operations and screen states, and evaluate whether the project scope is realistic.

AI Review Summary

The backlog was reviewed to check whether each module includes the necessary Create, Read, Update, and Delete (CRUD) operations. The review also considered important screen states such as list view, detail view, create form, edit form, empty state, error state, and delete confirmation.

The AI review suggested that the team should ensure that every CRUD operation has corresponding screens and states, especially empty states, error states, validation messages, and delete confirmation dialogs.

Scope Review

The project scope was evaluated based on the available development time and team size. The core features should remain focused on the main modules: Tenant, Room, Payment, and Maintenance Request. Additional features that are not essential to the main system should be avoided to prevent the project from becoming too large.

Team Decision

The team decided to prioritize the core CRUD functionality and required screen states first. Non-essential features will be rejected or postponed if they increase the project scope unnecessarily.

Conclusion

The AI review helped identify possible gaps in the backlog and reminded the team to design not only the successful or “happy path” screens but also empty, error, validation, and confirmation states. The final backlog will serve as the basis for the wireframes and development of the system.
